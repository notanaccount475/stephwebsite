About Me

Hi! I’m Stephanie and when I’m not messing with data, my hobbies include making art, playing the ukulele, volunteering, and obsessing over my Rubik’s cube time. Below you’ll find some quick facts about me, some photos of my life, some of my artwork, and a bit more about my volunteer experience.

Favorites

  • Favorite meal: 番茄炒蛋 (stir-fried tomato and egg). It's a classic comfort dish!
  • Favorite dessert: Tiramisu
  • Favorite city: Obviously NYC, where I live! But Chicago is a close second

On weekends, that period of time where it gets a little dusky but sunset hasn't actually occurred. To me, that signals the evening's fun is about to start. Plus it's a little sweetly melancholic...

Volunteering

Giving back to my community has always been incredibly important to me. I try my best to be of help wherever I can. Two of my favorite organizations to work with are New York Blood Center (I'm hitting my 1-gallon milestone soon!) and Columbia REAP, the ReEntry Acceleration Program that strives to improve employment opportunities for formerly incarcerated people.
